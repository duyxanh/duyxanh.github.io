About Me

Visual Computing Student

Hey :) I'm Duy Anh Nguyen, 23, based in Berlin. I study International Media and Computer Science at HTW Berlin, a programme that sits between design and engineering. That in-between is where I like to work.

My projects run from 3D graphics and software engineering to web development and media management. Different tools, same job: turning an idea into something you can see, click and use. That translation is the part I enjoy most.

This portfolio is a reflection of that journey. Every piece represents a different challenge, a new skill, or an idea I wanted to bring to life. Hope you will enjoy it!

Fun Fact

Passion For Dancing

In 2025 I competed on season 20 of America's Got Talent with my crew Team Recycled, and we made it to the finale. Performing on that stage, alongside Ciara, is still the biggest thing I have done in dance.

Alongside my studies I dance freelance: live shows, events and creative productions. The work is collaboration, with choreographers, artists and other dancers, to get a concept from an idea onto a stage.

Freelancing taught me the other half of it: managing my own projects, coordinating schedules and being accountable for what I commit to. That part transfers to everything else I do.
